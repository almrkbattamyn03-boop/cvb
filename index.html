Online Payment for Federal Tickets

Use this form to pay for a U.S. District Court Violation Notice. Please note that motor vehicle violations may be reported to the appropriate state agency and could affect your driving record. If you have any concerns regarding the impact, contact the Central Violations Bureau (CVB).

Important: You may pay the total collateral due in lieu of appearing in court if box 'B' is checked on your violation notice. You must appear in court if box 'A' is checked.

See a sample violation notice (PDF) for reference.

Fields marked with * are required.

Maximum four characters. Remove any spaces, dashes, or slashes.
Examples: 2, 2M, A95, FHTX, K5A

Include alphabetic characters if present. 7-8 character code.
Examples: E1056262, 09815570, FANTOOD
